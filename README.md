# The Missing Layer: A Standard for Day-to-Day AI Across GTM

A short governance framework for scaling AI adoption across GTM teams without
losing visibility, ownership, or control. Written after watching the same
pattern repeat: capable people building AI agents independently and well,
with no shared standard connecting the work back together.

**Read the deck:** [`the-missing-layer.pdf`](./the-missing-layer.pdf)

## The problem

AI adoption inside GTM orgs rarely fails because people can't build with AI.
It fails because dozens of capable people build in isolation, duplicating
effort, running unoptimized processes, with no shared definition of what's
safe, owned, or reusable.

## The model

Three layers, Champions, a Center of Excellence, and a Council, that give
fast, distributed AI building a governance layer without slowing it down:
direction flows down, evidence flows up.
